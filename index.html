      Setting it up safely

      • Set the diet filter to gluten-free and dairy-free, and pescatarian / poultry / plant — never beef, pork or lamb.
      • Services that are gluten-free and dairy-free by default (like Thistle) are the easiest, safest start. Plant-based alone isn't enough — bread, pasta and seitan are vegan.
      • Watch cream/cheese sauces, butter finishes, bread or roll sides, soy-sauce marinades and breaded items; leave out any cheese or bread a kit includes.
      • Check each recipe's allergen line for milk and wheat, and message support to flag the three allergies.

      My allergy card

      I have serious food allergies to red meat, milk and wheat.

      I cannot eat: Red meat: beef, pork, lamb, goat, veal, venison, bison — and broth, lard or gelatin made from them · Dairy: milk, butter, ghee, cheese, cream, yogurt, whey · Wheat: bread, buns, wraps, flour tortillas, pasta, noodles, breading, batter, croutons, soy sauce & teriyaki
      I can eat: Chicken, turkey, duck · fish & shellfish · eggs · rice, corn, quinoa, potatoes · fruit, vegetables, beans, nuts · anything labelled gluten-free and dairy-free
      Please tell me: Does this dish have any beef, pork or lamb (or their broth/lard)? Any milk, butter, cheese or cream? Any flour, bread, breading or soy sauce? Shared fryers and buttered grills matter too. Reactions can be severe.

      Show this to restaurant staff. Keep epinephrine nearby.

      If a reaction starts

      1. Stop eating immediately.
      2. Take antihistamine for mild symptoms (hives, itching).
      3. For trouble breathing, throat tightness, swelling, or faintness: use epinephrine now and call 911.
      4. Reactions can be delayed 2–6 hours — stay alert after eating something new.
