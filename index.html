        Label cheat-sheet

        If you see these words on a package:

          My allergy card

          I have Alpha-gal Syndrome — a serious allergy to mammalian meat and byproducts.

          I cannot eat: Beef, pork, lamb, goat, venison, bison & other mammal meat · gelatin · lard/tallow · beef/pork broth · collagen
          I can eat: Chicken, turkey, duck · fish & shellfish · eggs · all plant foods
          Please tell me: Are there any mammal products, shared grills, or beef/pork broths in this dish? Reactions can be delayed hours and severe.

          Show this to restaurant staff. Keep epinephrine nearby.

          If a reaction starts

          1. Stop eating immediately.
          2. Take antihistamine for mild symptoms (hives, itching).
          3. For trouble breathing, throat tightness, swelling, or faintness: use epinephrine now and call 911.
          4. Reactions can be delayed 2–6 hours — stay alert after eating something new.
